For Brokers & Agents

Life Settlement Brokers & Agents

If you have a client who is interested in or could benefit from a Life Settlement, we would be happy to work with you. The Settlement Group can work either directly with your client or as an extension of your team.

What we bring to the table

We offer personalized service that places high value on open communication. Our goal in this matter is complete transparency. That means we disclose all fees from the outset and will not require payment unless we sell your client's policy. We work to keep things as simple as possible for you and your clients.

As a direct buyer, our goal is to provide our partners with all the tools they'll need to evaluate a Life Settlement as a potential option for their clients. We can advise you of the policy's value, maximize that value in the market, locate buyers for the policy, help evaluate the offers and handle the vast majority of the paperwork.

Our in-house legal and compliance department is also on hand to ensure that the entire process is as free of complications as possible. You can rest assured that Settlement Group will be a capable partner who, like you, wants the best for your valued client.

Our simple yet effective online tools enable financial professionals to quickly identify and qualify potential Life Settlement cases. If a case is found to be qualified, our Life Settlement Calculator tool can be used to project an approximate market value on a particular policy.

For quick and efficient pricing and offers for any potential Life Settlement cases that you may have, please don't hesitate to contact us. Our dedicated team will be happy to look over your case and answer any questions.

Fulfilling our responsibilities

You are well familiar with the fact that as an agent or financial advisor, you have a fiduciary responsibility to advise your clients of their options. We, in turn, have a duty to protect your client's asset and secure the best possible offer for them.

If after reviewing the facts and case details with us, you and your client decide that a Life Settlement makes economic sense, we recommend partnering with the Settlement Group. By doing so, you will be aligning yourself with an experienced and talented team of life settlement professionals, tapping into established business relationships and partnerships, and adding a powerful tool to your financial portfolio.

The Settlement Group team is helping to guide the industry with best practices, and we pride ourselves on our high standard of values and ethics. Please contact us today and learn more about how we can help you to ensure that your client chooses the correct course of action for them, and makes prudent economic choices to protect their estate.
